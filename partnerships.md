# Plystra Partnerships

## Purpose

A Plystra partnership is a way for an excellent independent developer, or a very small studio, to build a product together with Plystra and release it worldwide. Plystra works on the product itself: design, code, writing, and review. Plystra LLC, a United States company, sells the product and collects payment from customers around the world. When the product meets the Plystra standard, it carries the Plystra name.

Plystra does this to learn from people who build well and to let Plystra grow through work it is proud of. It does not charge partners for it.

This document states what a partnership is, how one forms, who owns what, how the Plystra name is used, and how a partnership ends. It is the basis for written agreements; it is not itself an agreement.

## Current status

`Planned`. No partnership has been signed. The terms below describe the intended arrangement and must be reviewed by a lawyer and an accountant before the first agreement is signed; see [Before the first agreement](#before-the-first-agreement).

## What a partnership is

A partnership is a relationship between Plystra LLC and one author: an individual developer or a studio of a few people. Plystra does not partner with larger companies, does not invest, and does not take equity.

The partnership differs from the relationships defined in [Plystra Craft](https://github.com/plystra/craft#scope-and-project-relationships):

| Relationship | Who owns the product | Plystra's role | Public wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plystra-owned project | Plystra LLC | Owner and steward | A Plystra project. |
| Sponsored project | Its independent owner | Agreed support only | Sponsored by Plystra. |
| **Partner project** | **The author** | **Co-developer and seller** | **Made by [Author] with Plystra.** |

A partner project is not a Plystra-owned project and must not be described as one. Plystra's work on it is deeper than sponsorship: Plystra takes part in product decisions and in the work itself, and the product carries the Plystra name once it has earned it.

## What each side contributes

Plystra contributes its time and judgment. It works on the product alongside the author, including design, engineering, writing, naming, and release preparation, and it takes part in product decisions throughout the partnership. Plystra LLC acts as the seller: it sells the product to customers worldwide, collects payment, and pays the author. Plystra charges no fee, commission, or revenue share. The author receives the proceeds less only third-party costs, such as payment processing, platform fees, and taxes that must be withheld by law.

The author contributes the product and remains responsible for it. The author maintains the product, answers for its code and conduct, and shares all of its source with Plystra, as described in [Source sharing](#source-sharing).

## How a partnership forms

A partnership forms in stages, and either side may stop at any stage before an agreement is signed.

1. **Conversation.** Plystra and the author talk at length about the product, how the author works, and what each side expects. Plystra partners with people before it partners with products; a strong product from someone Plystra does not trust does not proceed.
2. **Trial collaboration.** Plystra and the author work together on one concrete piece, such as a feature or a release, for two to four weeks. The trial shows how each side writes code, handles feedback, keeps commitments, and resolves disagreement.
3. **Written agreement.** Both sides sign an agreement that records the terms in this document, the Craft provisions the product must meet, and anything specific to the product.
4. **Co-development.** Plystra and the author work on the product together. The product uses its own name and makes no reference to Plystra.
5. **Standard review.** Plystra's steward reviews the product against the agreed standard. If it meets the standard, it may carry the Plystra name. If it does not, work continues or the partnership ends, and the public never learns of the association.
6. **Release and continuing review.** Plystra LLC sells the product. The standard continues to apply to every release, and Plystra may review the product at any time.

Plystra takes on only as many partnerships as it can work on deeply. A candidate may be asked to wait.

## Ownership

The author owns the product. The copyright in the product's code, design, and content belongs to the author, and the product's domain is registered to the author.

Everything Plystra contributes to the product, including code, designs, writing, and ideas, also belongs to the author. Plystra assigns its rights in those contributions to the author and keeps no claim to them.

Two things are excluded from that assignment. First, materials Plystra created outside the partnership and brings into it, such as Plystra's own libraries, tools, and components, remain Plystra's; the author receives a license to use them in the product. Second, the Plystra name, wordmark, and visual identity remain Plystra's and are licensed to the product only as described in [Using the Plystra name](#using-the-plystra-name).

## Source sharing

The author shares all source with Plystra for the whole partnership. This includes the product itself, its official website, and any service, tool, or script the product depends on. Plystra may read, review, and change all of it.

Plystra keeps its own copy of the source, under its own control, and updates it continuously. Access must not depend on the author's continued permission during the partnership, so that a disagreement cannot remove Plystra's ability to see what is being sold under its name.

The author keeps the domain in good standing for the whole partnership, with automatic renewal and registrar lock enabled.

## Using the Plystra name

The Plystra name is attached only to a product that meets the Plystra standard, and only after Plystra's steward confirms it in writing. Before that, the product carries no Plystra name, mark, or reference anywhere.

Once confirmed, the product may say:

> Made by [Author] with Plystra.

The product's own name stays first. The author is credited by name on the product's site and in the product. Receipts and store listings name Plystra LLC as the seller. No surface may describe the product as a Plystra-owned project or as a Plystra project.

The license to use the Plystra name ends when the partnership ends or when Plystra withdraws it.

## Decisions

The author and Plystra make product decisions together. When they disagree about the product itself, such as its features, roadmap, or technical approach, the author decides.

Plystra decides anything that carries the Plystra name. Plystra may withhold or withdraw the name from any product or release, and it may refuse to sell a release, on grounds of quality, privacy, security, honesty of claims, or fit with the Plystra brand. Plystra does not need the author's agreement to do so.

## Ending a partnership

Plystra may end a partnership at any time, immediately and without the author's agreement. Grounds include, without limitation:

- unlawful conduct connected with the product or the author;
- infringement, plagiarism, or a license violation in the product;
- malicious code, undisclosed data collection, or a concealed security weakness;
- a release that falls below the agreed standard and is not corrected;
- public conduct by the author that harms Plystra's reputation;
- any other circumstance in which Plystra judges that continuing would harm the Plystra brand.

The author may end a partnership with 30 days' written notice.

When a partnership ends, Plystra LLC stops selling the product, and the author removes the Plystra name and every Plystra mark from the product, its website, and its listings within 7 days. The author may not describe the product as formerly made with Plystra or otherwise refer to the past association unless Plystra agrees in writing. The product keeps its own name and continues under the author.

Customers who paid through Plystra LLC are not abandoned. The agreement provides for refunds or a transition to the author's own sales, and for the handling of customer data, before the partnership ends. Proceeds owed to the author up to the end date are paid in full.

## Acquisition

Plystra does not promise to acquire any partner product. Acquisition is possible, but no conversation and no partnership commits Plystra to it, and Plystra does not suggest otherwise to win a partnership.

The agreement gives Plystra a right of first negotiation. If the author decides to sell the product, the author first negotiates with Plystra in good faith for an agreed period before offering it to anyone else.

## Payments and tax

Plystra LLC is the seller of record, either directly or through a merchant-of-record platform that handles sales tax and VAT. Every payment to an author follows a written agreement, and every author completes the tax forms United States law requires, such as Form W-8BEN for a foreign individual. Plystra LLC withholds tax where the law requires it. Each author is responsible for tax and currency rules in their own country.

Plystra does not collect or move money informally on anyone's behalf.

## Before the first agreement

The following must be settled with a lawyer and an accountant before the first agreement is signed:

- the form of the agreement, including the assignment of Plystra's contributions, the license of Plystra's existing materials, the brand license, termination, and the right of first negotiation;
- the author's warranties about originality, licensing, and security, and the author's responsibility for breaches of them;
- whether partner products should be sold through Plystra LLC or through a separate entity that isolates their liabilities;
- the choice of merchant-of-record platform, refund handling, and chargebacks;
- tax treatment of payments to authors, including withholding and treaty rates;
- customer data: who controls it during the partnership and what happens to it at the end.
