# Plystra Docs

Documents that describe how Plystra works with people outside it.

## Documents

- [Plystra Partnerships](partnerships.md): how Plystra makes products together with independent product creators and very small studios, states its participation once a product meets the Plystra standard, and, only if the author wants it, sells the product worldwide. `Planned`.
- [Plystra Membership](membership.md): how a product creator who has proven themselves in a partnership becomes part of Plystra, what membership is and is not, and how it ends. `Planned`.
