# Plystra Handbook

How Plystra works with people: the terms of each relationship Plystra enters into, and how those terms are carried out in practice.

[Plystra Craft](https://github.com/plystra/craft) defines what good work is. This handbook defines how Plystra relates to the people who make it, and adds no standards of its own.

## Relationships

- [Plystra Partnerships](partnerships.md): how Plystra makes products together with independent product creators and very small studios, when a product becomes a partner project, and, only if the author wants it, how Plystra sells it worldwide. `Planned`.
- [Plystra Membership](membership.md): how a product creator who has proven themselves in a partnership becomes part of Plystra, what membership is and is not, and how it ends. `Planned`.
