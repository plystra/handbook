# Plystra Handbook

Who Plystra is, and how it relates to the people and projects around it: which projects are Plystra's own, how Plystra makes products with partners, what it sponsors, who becomes part of it, and who may use its name.

[Plystra Craft](https://github.com/plystra/craft) defines what good work is. This handbook defines everything about Plystra as an organization, and adds no standards for the work itself.

## Contents

```text
plystra/handbook
├── README.md
├── LICENSE
├── charter.md                  # why Plystra exists and what it aims to become
├── stewardship.md              # who decides, how governance grows, who may act for Plystra
├── brand-use.md                # who may use the Plystra name and marks
└── relationships/
    ├── README.md               # the three project relationships, members, and public wording
    ├── plystra-projects.md     # projects Plystra owns
    ├── partnerships.md         # products Plystra makes together with their authors
    ├── sponsorship.md          # projects Plystra chooses to support
    └── membership.md           # people who are part of Plystra
```

- [Charter](charter.md): Plystra's identity, ambition, and promise.
- [Stewardship](stewardship.md): the steward, governance maturity, written authorizations, and records.
- [Brand Use](brand-use.md): who may use the Plystra name and on what basis.
- [Relationships](relationships/README.md): Plystra projects, partner projects, sponsored projects, and members.
  - [Plystra Projects](relationships/plystra-projects.md): what a Plystra project is, admission, and retirement.
  - [Plystra Partnerships](relationships/partnerships.md): how a product becomes a partner project, and, only if the author wants it, how Plystra sells it. `Planned`.
  - [Sponsorship](relationships/sponsorship.md): support Plystra gives at its discretion, and when it may be stated.
  - [Plystra Membership](relationships/membership.md): how a proven partner author becomes part of Plystra. `Planned`.
