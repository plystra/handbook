# Plystra Membership

## Purpose

Plystra membership is how a product creator becomes part of Plystra. Members are people whose own work Plystra has come to know and trust, and who choose to stay close to Plystra: to exchange ideas with other excellent product creators around the world, to give and receive serious criticism, and to take part in shaping products they care about.

Membership pays nothing and asks for nothing. A member joins because being part of Plystra makes them a better creator, and stays only as long as that remains true.

This document states who may become a member, what membership is and is not, how a member may speak about Plystra, and how membership ends. It is the basis for written membership terms; it is not itself an agreement.

## Current status

`Planned`. Plystra has no members. Membership depends on [partnerships](partnerships.md), and no partnership has been signed.

## Who may become a member

Only the author of a partner product may become a member. Membership begins with work, not with an application: a person first builds a product together with Plystra as described in [Plystra Partnerships](partnerships.md), and shows through that work that they are reliable, principled, and able to give and take criticism well.

A product creator who has proven themselves in a partnership may become a member when both they and Plystra want it. Membership is agreed between them; it is never automatic, and it is never a condition of a partnership or a reward promised to win one. Plystra's steward decides whether to offer membership and may decline without giving reasons.

Plystra is a home for products, not for technical projects. Members are chosen for the strength of their products and their judgment: finding problems worth solving, forming independent views on experience, design, and business, and carrying an idea through to a complete and reliable product, whoever or whatever writes the code.

## What membership is

A member may:

- exchange ideas with other members and with partner authors;
- offer views on products in partnership with Plystra, when the product's author invites them;
- bring their own products and ideas for discussion and criticism;
- describe themselves publicly as part of Plystra, as described in [Speaking about membership](#speaking-about-membership).

How much time a member gives, and to what, is entirely the member's choice. Membership carries no expectation of continuing work, and a member who is quiet for a long time is still a member.

## What membership is not

Membership is not employment. A member is not an employee, contractor, or agent of Plystra LLC, receives no pay from it, and owes it no work.

Membership is not authority. A member cannot sign for Plystra LLC, make commitments on its behalf, decide for Plystra, or speak as Plystra, except as described in [Acting for Plystra](#acting-for-plystra).

Membership is not ownership. A member holds no share in Plystra LLC, no claim to its assets, and no right to the Plystra name beyond what this document permits.

Membership does not change a partnership. A member who is also a partner author has exactly the rights and obligations their partnership agreement gives them, no more and no fewer.

## Acting for Plystra

By default, no member has any authority to act for Plystra. Plystra's steward may authorize a particular member, in writing, to make particular decisions or to speak for Plystra in particular matters. Each authorization states its scope and its duration, and Plystra may withdraw it at any time.

Plystra keeps a current record of every authorization, so that anyone dealing with a member can confirm what that member may and may not do for Plystra. Anything outside a written authorization is the member's own act, not Plystra's.

## Contributions and ideas

When a member contributes to another creator's product, who owns the contribution is settled between the member and that product's author. Plystra LLC is not a party to that arrangement, takes no part in it, and claims nothing from it. Ideas that members exchange, with each other or with partner authors, never belong to Plystra.

Plystra does not share a partner author's confidential information, such as unreleased source or plans, with members. Anything an author chooses to show a member is shown on terms the author and the member agree between themselves.

## Speaking about membership

A member may say that they are part of Plystra, for example:

> I'm part of Plystra.

> [Name], a member of Plystra.

This is a current endorsement of the person, in the sense of [current endorsement and historical attribution](partnerships.md#current-endorsement-and-historical-attribution), and lasts only while the membership does. It must not suggest that the member is employed by Plystra LLC, acts for it, or speaks for it, unless a written authorization says so.

Membership says nothing about a member's other products. A member's own product carries a statement of Plystra's participation only through a partnership, as described in [Plystra Partnerships](partnerships.md#stating-plystras-participation).

## Ending membership

A member may leave at any time, for any reason, by telling Plystra.

Plystra may end a membership at any time, at its own discretion, because a member's conduct reflects on the Plystra name as much as a product does. Plystra explains its reasons to the member unless the reason is serious cause.

Membership does not end merely because a partnership ends. A member whose partnership ended in the ordinary way remains a member unless either side ends the membership. A partnership that ends for serious cause ends the membership at the same time.

When a membership ends, the person removes every current claim to be part of Plystra within 3 days. After a member leaves, or after Plystra ends a membership for any reason other than serious cause, the person may make a truthful, past-tense statement such as "Part of Plystra from 2027 to 2029". After an end for serious cause, no such statement may be made on any current surface.
