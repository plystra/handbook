# Plystra Projects

## 1. What a Plystra project is

A Plystra project is a project owned and stewarded by Plystra LLC. Plystra is responsible for it in full.

A Plystra project may be named after Plystra, such as "Plystra Auth", or carry a name of its own. A distinct name, domain, technical stack, business model, or maturity level does not change what the project is or what it owes. A project with a name of its own may keep the Plystra name off its own brand surfaces where its identity calls for it; its records and its relationship statement still say that it is a Plystra project.

## 2. Admission

A project becomes a Plystra project only when the steward admits it, and only if it meets all of these conditions:

1. It expresses a long-term idea, not merely a one-off implementation.
2. It can be maintained with available resources.
3. It benefits from being understood as part of the Plystra family.
4. It can be explained without relying on hype.
5. It has a plausible path to documentation, support, and responsible stewardship.
6. It commits to the full Plystra Craft philosophy, with clear responsibility for meeting its requirements.

A partner product that meets these conditions may also become a Plystra project by transfer, as described in [Becoming a Plystra project](partnerships.md#becoming-a-plystra-project).

## 3. Obligations

A Plystra project follows the full [Plystra Craft](https://github.com/plystra/craft) philosophy and keeps the [project principles record](https://github.com/plystra/craft/blob/main/ADOPTION.md#4-project-principles) it requires. Its records name Plystra LLC as owner, and its public attribution matches them.

## 4. Wording

A Plystra project can say:

> A Plystra project.

## 5. Retirement

Retiring a Plystra project is acceptable. Abandoning one silently is not. A retired project follows the [retirement requirements](https://github.com/plystra/craft/blob/main/principles/10-release-and-maintenance.md#9-retirement) in Craft.
