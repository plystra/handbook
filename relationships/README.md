# Relationships

Plystra has three kinds of relationship with a project and one with a person. This document defines them, the public wording each permits, and what is not a relationship at all.

## Project relationships

One question decides a project's relationship to Plystra: who owns it.

| Relationship | Who owns it | What Plystra gives | How the Plystra name may appear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Plystra project](plystra-projects.md) | Plystra LLC | Everything: Plystra owns and is responsible for it | Always, as a Plystra project |
| [Partner project](partnerships.md) | Its author | Shaping the product together, and selling it if the author wants | Only with Plystra's written consent, after a standard review; Plystra may withdraw it |
| [Sponsored project](sponsorship.md) | Its independent owner | Whatever support Plystra chooses to give | Only with a separate written consent; Plystra may withdraw it |

There is one path between them: a partner product may become a Plystra project when both sides want it, as described in [Becoming a Plystra project](partnerships.md#becoming-a-plystra-project).

## People

A [member](membership.md) is a person, not a project. Only a product creator who has proven themselves in a partnership may become one.

## Public wording

| Relationship | Wording |
| --- | --- |
| Plystra project | A Plystra project. |
| Partner project | A statement of Plystra's participation agreed in writing for the product, such as "Made with Plystra." |
| Sponsored project | Sponsored by Plystra. |
| Member | I'm part of Plystra. |

Public wording must match the steward's [records](../stewardship.md#5-records) and must keep the relationships distinct. A partner project or a sponsored project is never described as a Plystra project, never listed among Plystra's own projects, and never presented as owned, operated, or guaranteed by Plystra. When a relationship ends, current wording is removed; whether a truthful, past-tense statement may remain is set by the document for that relationship.

## What is not a relationship

- **Exchange.** Talking with Plystra about a product, receiving its criticism, or exchanging ideas with it does not create any relationship.
- **Using Craft or this handbook.** Anyone may use them under their licenses. Doing so creates no relationship and permits no claim of endorsement or compliance; see [Brand Use](../brand-use.md).
- **A shared author.** A project does not become a Plystra project because it was made by someone who also works on Plystra. It carries the Plystra name only because it shares the same care, restraint, and long-term direction, and the steward has admitted it.
