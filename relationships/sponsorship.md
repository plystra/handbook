# Sponsorship

## 1. What sponsorship is

A sponsored project is an independently owned project that receives support from Plystra. Its owner keeps full ownership and control of it, and Plystra takes no part in its decisions unless both sides agree otherwise.

Sponsorship is given at the steward's discretion. Plystra decides whom to sponsor, what to give, and for how long. The support may be money, services, resources, or anything else the steward chooses. There is no application, admission list, or standard review, and no project is entitled to sponsorship.

## 2. Sponsorship is not endorsement

Sponsoring a project says that Plystra chose to support it, not that the project meets the Plystra standard. A sponsored project need not follow Plystra Craft, though it may adopt any part of it.

For the same reason, sponsorship does not by itself let the project use the Plystra name. Plystra may sponsor a project quietly.

## 3. Stating the sponsorship

A sponsored project may state the sponsorship publicly only with Plystra's separate written consent. The consent names where the statement may appear, and Plystra may withdraw it at any time. The statement is:

> Sponsored by Plystra.

It may be followed by a plain description of the support, such as "Hosting is sponsored by Plystra." It must describe the support truthfully and must not suggest that Plystra owns, operates, guarantees, or endorses the project, or that the project is a Plystra project or a partner project.

## 4. Ending sponsorship

Plystra may end a sponsorship at any time. The owner may decline further support at any time.

When the sponsorship or the consent to state it ends, the project removes current sponsorship statements within 3 days. A truthful, past-tense acknowledgment, such as "Hosting was sponsored by Plystra in 2027", may remain unless Plystra's written consent excluded it.
