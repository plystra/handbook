# Stewardship

This document states who decides for Plystra, how Plystra's governance is meant to grow, and who may act on Plystra's behalf.

## 1. The steward

Plystra is stewarded by Plystra LLC. The steward is responsible for Plystra's direction, naming, and public standards, and for every relationship described in [Relationships](relationships/README.md): which projects are Plystra's own, which products become partner projects, what Plystra sponsors, and who becomes a member.

## 2. Governance philosophy

Plystra should grow governance gradually.

Do not imitate the full structure of a foundation before there is a real contributor base, maintenance burden, or legal need. Plystra is a small company, Plystra LLC, and should be treated as a craft umbrella first: careful, independent, and not prematurely institutionalized. But do build habits that make future governance possible, as described in the rest of this document.

## 3. Governance maturity

Plystra governance may evolve through stages:

### Stage 0: Company stewardship

Plystra LLC controls direction. Decisions are still documented.

### Stage 1: Maintainer-led projects

Projects may have contributors. Maintainer roles and review expectations are documented.

### Stage 2: Shared project governance

Multiple maintainers can approve changes. Release and security responsibilities are assigned.

### Stage 3: Foundation-like stewardship

Project admission, retirement, trademark use, and governance are formalized.

Do not skip stages for appearance.

## 4. Acting for Plystra

By default, nobody other than the steward has authority to act for Plystra. Not a member, a partner author, a sponsored project, or a contributor to a Plystra project may sign for Plystra LLC, make commitments on its behalf, decide for Plystra, or speak as Plystra.

The steward may authorize a particular person, in writing, to make particular decisions or to speak for Plystra in particular matters. Each authorization states its scope and its duration, and the steward may withdraw it at any time.

Plystra keeps a current record of every authorization, so that anyone dealing with a person can confirm what that person may and may not do for Plystra. Anything outside a written authorization is the person's own act, not Plystra's.

## 5. Records

The steward records each consequential decision about a relationship in writing: admitting a Plystra project, consenting to or withdrawing from a partnership, starting or ending a sponsorship, offering or ending a membership, and granting or withdrawing an authorization. Public descriptions of a relationship must match these records.
